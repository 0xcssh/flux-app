Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log my daily check-in?

Tap the "+" button in the center of the tab bar. Adjust the 6 sliders to reflect how you feel today, then tap "Save Today's Log". It takes less than 60 seconds.

What is the Vitality Score?

Your Vitality Score (0-100) is a weighted average of your 6 daily metrics: energy, mood, libido, sleep quality, stress (inverted), and physical activity. It gives you a quick snapshot of your overall state.

Do I need an account?

No. Flux works fully without an account. All your data is stored locally on your device. You can optionally create an account to sync your data across devices.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Flux Premium > Cancel Subscription. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Flux > Profile > tap "Upgrade Plan" > tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the paywall screen.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your health data stays on your device. We do not sell or share personal data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

What is the circadian rhythm?

Your body's 24-hour internal clock. Testosterone peaks in the early morning and declines throughout the day. Flux maps this rhythm so you can plan your day around your biology.
